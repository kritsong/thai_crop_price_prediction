# Author confirmation checklist

The files in `paper/` are technically ready for final review. Before uploading
them to Editorial Manager, the corresponding author must personally confirm
the statements below; they cannot be established from code or public records.

- [ ] Every author approves the manuscript, author order, submission, and cover letter.
- [ ] The CRediT roles in `paper/final_paper.md` accurately describe each author's contribution.
- [ ] The no-specific-grant funding statement is accurate; otherwise add the funder and grant number.
- [ ] The competing-interest declaration is accurate for every author.
- [ ] The manuscript is original and is not under review or published elsewhere.
- [ ] The data/code availability statement matches what the corresponding author will provide on request.
- [ ] The declaration of AI-assisted tools is complete and acceptable to all authors.
- [ ] Corresponding-author email and postal address are current; add a telephone number in Editorial Manager if requested.
- [ ] Optional ORCID identifiers have been collected and entered in Editorial Manager.
- [ ] The cover-letter date is refreshed if submission occurs after 31 July 2026.
- [ ] The journal's current author guide and submission questions are reviewed immediately before upload.
- [x] The double-spaced review PDF is at the KBS guide's stated 20-page preference; detailed diagnostics and secondary analyses are preserved in the supplementary PDF.

Suggested upload set: `paper/main.tex`, the 12 files in `paper/images/`,
`paper/main.pdf` for review, `paper/supplementary.tex`,
`paper/supplementary.pdf`, `paper/cover_letter.pdf`, and
`paper/highlights.txt`. Upload editable source where the submission system asks
for source files and classify the supplementary PDF as supplementary material.
